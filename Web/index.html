Admission Requirements

  • Admission to the chosen degree program will be based on an applicant's Gordon College Admission Test (GCAT) results and previous high school/college academic records.
  • Original copy of High School Report Card (F138/SF9) or Original ALS Certificate of Rating Secondary Level for ALS Passers.
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character.
  • Copy of Birth Certificate.
  • Medical Certificate (Issued by the Gordon College Clinic).
  • Barangay Certificate of Residency.
  • One 2x2 Picture.

About Gordon College

Freshmen Students

Welcome to Gordon College! As a new student, you'll find a supportive campus community, dedicated mentors, and opportunities to grow academically, socially, and personally.

Vision

A globally recognized local institution committed to innovative academic excellence, holistic and sustainable development, inclusivity, and community engagement.

Mission

Produce empowered global citizens who create sustainable impact, uphold values of character, excellence, and service, and contribute to academic and societal development.

Strategic Objectives

  1. Harness partnerships and linkages to produce graduates with global competencies aligned with the needs of the community and industry.
  2. Organize extension programs to sustain the greater impact of the institution on the community and environment.
  3. Leverage research outputs to improve existing knowledge and practices.
  4. Integrate ethical principles and standards in the curriculum to strengthen the moral responsibilities of lifelong learners.
  5. Support continuous professional development and the well-being of employees.
  6. Tailor-fit student support services to ensure students thrive in a rapidly changing world.
  7. Invest in state-of-the-art facilities and technologies to enhance teaching, learning, and research.
  8. Cultivate a culture that embraces diversity and inclusivity.

Core Values

Character, Excellence, Service.

School Rules & Policies

This Policy aims to:

  1. Protect everyone on campus
  2. Keep prohibited items out
  3. Maintain peace & order
  4. Regulate visitor access
  5. Guide emergency response
  6. Promote shared responsibility

Campus Access Control for Students

  • Must have a validated GC ID card
  • Wear ID visibly at the center chest
  • NO ID = NO ENTRY IS STRICTLY ENFORCED
  • Do not alter or tamper with your ID
  • Never lend or borrow someone else's ID

Campus Access Control for Visitors

  • Present a valid government-issued ID
  • Register in the visitor's logbook
  • Wear your visitor's pass at all times
  • State your purpose and who you're visiting
  • NON-COMPLIANCE = DENIED ENTRY

Bag Inspection Policy

All persons entering campus are subject to security inspection.

How we inspect:

  • Visual bag check: Security personnel physically inspect bags and personal belongings.
  • Handheld metal detector: Used to detect concealed metallic items on persons or inside bags.
  • Other authorized methods: Additional non-intrusive security measures as deemed necessary.

Reminder:

SECURITY INSPECTIONS MAY BE CONDUCTED ANYTIME, ANYWHERE ON CAMPUS WITHOUT PRIOR NOTICE.
 REFUSAL MEANS IMMEDIATE DENIED ENTRY OR RESTRICTED FACILITY ACCESS, WITH NO EXCEPTIONS.
